# Episode 60: Friendship of the Envious : The Hostel Lessons
### The Psychology of Rivalry and the Art of Forgiving the Bitter

**Season 6 : The Philosophical Treatise** | Words: 234

> **Synopsis:** Analyzing the roots of female-on-female jealousy in competitive educational settings, offering a roadmap for maintaining emotional grace when attacked by peers.

---

One of the most agonizing lessons of my university years was observing how intensely young women can attack other young women.
 
Why did Umme Rubab hate me with such violent, destructive intensity?
 
I had never spoken an unkind word to her; I had never competed for her friends; I had never touched a single coin of her family's wealth.
 
I came to realize that my offense in her eyes was not what I did, but *who I was*.
 
My mere existence : an impoverished village girl studying on a broken desk, wearing second-hand shoes, yet out-thinking and out-writing her on pure merit : was an unbearable, living mirror that exposed the utter vanity and hollow emptiness of her unearned privilege.
 
Envy is the tribute that mediocrity pays to excellence.
 
When a person cannot rise to your level through their own labor, their sole remaining instinct is to drag you down to their level through slander.
 
I hold no hatred for Umme Rubab today. In many ways, she was my greatest teacher.
 
Her slanders forced me into the isolation of the library; her attacks forced me to verify every single citation in my research; her cruelty taught me the priceless art of emotional detachment.
 
Do not curse your rivals. Bless them. For in their frantic attempts to dig your grave, they often dig the very trench that protects you from the arrows of the world.
