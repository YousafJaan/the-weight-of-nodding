# Episode 13: The Hookah of Taya Hakim Khan
### The Feudal Panchayat, The Silver Hookah, and The Decreed Sacrifice

**Season 2 : The Courtyard of Chains** | Words: 980

> **Synopsis:** Summoned before the patriarchal family tribunal, Rija faces Taya Hakim Khan, who decrees an immediate forced marriage to his illiterate, delinquent son Arslan to neutralize her education and preserve feudal control.

---

The formal drawing room : the *baithak* : was the sanctum sanctorum of our family's patriarchal authority. It was a long, dim room with bare whitewashed walls adorned only with faded hunting photographs, a mounted deer head whose glass eyes were clouded with dust, and a vintage twelve-gauge shotgun resting across two brass pegs.
 
The air inside was thick and suffocating with the sweet, acrid smoke of molasses tobacco burning inside Taya Hakim Khan's heirloom silver hookah. The rhythmic, wet gurgling of the hookah pipe sounded like the breathing of a prehistoric beast lurking in the shadows.
 
Seated upon the large central charpai, leaning against a cylindrical bolster cushion of crimson silk, was Taya Hakim Khan. At seventy years old, with a flowing, henna-dyed beard and a mountainous white turban wound tightly around his head, he commanded our extended agrarian clan with tyrannical absolute authority. Beside him sat my father, Sajjad, looking shrunken, exhausted, his white kurta wrinkled, his head lowered in profound humiliation.
 
In the far corner, perched on a wooden stool like an eager vulture, sat Aunt Shabana, her sharp features gleaming with malicious anticipation.
 
"Bring the girl forward," Taya Hakim Khan commanded without removing the silver mouthpiece from his lips.
 
Hamza shoved me from behind, propelling me into the center of the room. I stood upright, my black chador wrapped tightly around my body, my degree certificate held firmly inside my canvas bag.
 
Taya Hakim Khan took a slow, deliberate draw from the pipe, releasing a dense plume of grey smoke toward the ceiling rafters before fixing his cold, hooded eyes upon me.
 
"Sajjad," the patriarch began, his voice a deep, gravelly rasp that resonated through the floorboards. "For two years, you have subjected this family to public disgrace by allowing your daughter to wander among stranger men in the city of Multan. The village elders have come to my doorstep three times this month, asking if Sajjad's household has forgotten the meaning of *ghairat* : ancestral honor. We have tolerated your weakness only because you are my younger brother."
 
Father shifted uncomfortably, his voice barely audible : "Brother, she has completed her studies with highest honors. Dr. Masood told me..."
 
"Silence!" Taya Hakim Khan barked, slamming his silver hookah tongs onto the wooden tray with a deafening clang that made Father flinch. "I did not summon this council to listen to the opinions of city professors! What is a woman's honor? Is it written upon pieces of imported paper? No! A woman's honor exists only when her neck is bent beneath the yoke of her lawful master!"
 
He turned his piercing gaze back to me, his lip curling in undisguised contempt.
 
"The elders have rendered our verdict," Taya Hakim Khan announced, each syllable falling like a stone slab. "Next Friday, following the congregational prayer, your nikah will be solemnized with my younger son, Arslan. The wedding will be conducted quietly inside our compound. After the ceremony, you will surrender all your books and papers to Hamza to be burned in the hearth. You will remain within Arslan's four walls and never speak of this university madness again."
 
The room fell into an icy, petrified silence, broken only by the crackle of burning tobacco.

The decreed groom was Arslan : Taya's twenty-eight-year-old son, an illiterate, violent brute who had failed his matriculation examination three times, spent his days gambling on illegal quail fights at the canal crossing, and his nights stumbling through village alleys in drunken stupors.
 
The design behind this forced union was transparent in its feudal savagery : by chaining an educated, independent woman to an illiterate, violent cousin, the clan would permanently crush her spirit, neutralize her legal knowledge, and ensure that our family's five acres of ancestral canal land remained locked within Taya Hakim Khan's direct control.
 
Aunt Shabana leaned forward, her voice dripping with artificial sweetness : "Say *Alhamdulillah*, Rija! Taya ji is showing immense generosity. After all the scandalous rumors circulating about your hostel life, what respectable outside family would ever accept you? Arslan is your own blood cousin; he will cover your shame."
 
Every drop of blood inside my body surged into my temples. I looked at my father. He sat with his hands clasped between his knees, staring at the floorboards, refusing to meet my pleading gaze. The father who had promised to protect his daughter was paralyzed by the terror of his elder brother's disapproval.
 
I stepped forward into the clear space between the two charpais.
 
"Taya Hakim Khan," I said, my voice cutting through the smoke-filled room with crystal, uncompromising clarity. "Your son Arslan is a gambler, a delinquent, and an illiterate abuser who cannot write his own name. I have earned a postgraduate degree in political science and constitutional law with first-class distinction."
 
The room gasped. Taya's hookah pipe froze mid-air.
 
"Under the injunctions of the Holy Quran and the statutory laws of Pakistan," I continued, looking straight into the old patriarch's furious eyes, "a marriage without the free, uncoerced consent of the adult woman is void, illegal, and null. I do not consent. I will never consent. You may burn this entire village to the ground, but you will never force my thumb onto a nikahnama with your son."
 
For five seconds, the silence was so profound that the buzzing of a fly against the windowpane sounded like a thunderclap.
 
Then, all hell broke loose.
 
Taya Hakim Khan's face turned a dangerous, mottled purple. He kicked the brass hookah tray aside, sending hot coals scattering across the floor. "You insolent, cursed bitch!" he roared, his voice shaking with apoplectic rage. "Hamza! Take this shameless wretch away before I blow her brains out with my own hands!"
 
Hamza lunged forward, grabbed my hair with both fists, and violently dragged me out of the drawing room across the courtyard gravel as my screams echoed into the twilight.
